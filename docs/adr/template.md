## Context

What situation forces a decision? State the constraints (technical, project
goals, prior ADRs) and the forces in tension. Write it so a reader who knows
nothing about the discussion understands why "do nothing" was not an option.
Facts only — no advocacy here.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one or two declarative sentences ("We will …"), followed
by as much elaboration as the decision needs: scope, rules, and any diagrams.
Make rules explicit and checkable; a decision nobody can verify compliance with
is an opinion.

### Alternatives considered

For each serious alternative: what it was, its genuine advantages, and the
specific reason it lost. Be fair to the losers — this section is what keeps the
decision from being re-litigated every six months.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what risks we knowingly accept —
including the follow-up work the decision creates. Honest downsides belong here;
an ADR with no negative consequences hasn't found them yet.
